# W3LTFRIEDEN

## Digital Infrastructure for Global Peace

A global, decentralized, grassroots-democratic platform that translates personal commitment into concrete,
scalable peace missions powered by ethical AI.

### Vision

In seven years, 95% of the world's population lives in peace. A global, decentralized, participatory order
with mandatory diplomacy and consensus forms the foundation of collective decision-making.

### The Problem & Solution

**The Problem:** Millions of people engage for peace, yet their efforts remain fragmented, invisible, and
powerless against global crises.

**The Solution:** An operational peace infrastructure where AI serves ethically to translate personal
commitment directly into concrete, local, and scalable peace missions.

### Core Operating System

1. **Activation**: Guides people from intention into concrete roles
2. **Missions**: Generates, prioritizes, and curates peace missions
3. **Resources**: Pools time, knowledge, networks, and capital
4. **Cooperations**: Connects actors systemically beyond isolated projects
5. **Impact**: Makes progress and impact measurable
6. **Visibility**: Makes peace builders and teams discoverable

### Governance Framework

1. Life, freedom, and integrity are inviolable
2. Peace is the path. Violence is not an option
3. Technology serves life, not control
4. Free access to information and knowledge for all
5. Mandatory human-in-the-loop: People always bear final responsibility for AI-driven decisions
